Rendered standalone by RecibosResource action endpoints (primary swap target #recibos-tablero) and included by pages/recibos/index.html. Model keys: stats RecibosResource.StatsView (todas/pagadas/procesadas/vencidos) modelos Map bucket-key -> kit data-table model (tablaModel) toastSeverity/toastMessage - optional OOB toast after an action Bucket queries mirror the legacy pages/Recibos/index.xhtml tabs onto the emitted-comprobante lifecycle (see RecibosResource javadoc): todas = all, pagadas = Encabezado.estado ACEPTADO, procesadas = Hacienda exchange done, vencidos = unsettled credit past due. ONE BUCKET ON SCREEN -------------------- The four tables are four views of ONE dataset. Rendering all four made the board two screens tall, so the clerk could not tell which bucket they were reading and read all of them. The board now shows one bucket at a time: _kit/view-tabs carries the counts (the old anchor "KPI" cards were neither filters nor tabs - they were a jump list) and the Alpine `bucket` variable picks the visible panel. The tab labels are the SAME words the tables use for their own heading (Todas / Pagadas / Procesadas / Vencidos), so the mapping is learnable. The `bucket` variable lives in the Alpine scope of pages/recibos/index.html - one level up, outside this swap target. A row action re-renders the whole board; a scope declared inside the board would be rebuilt with it and the operator would land back on the first bucket. !}
