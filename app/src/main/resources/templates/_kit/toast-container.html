{! _kit/toast-container - the ONE toast mount point (W1). EXTRACTED VERBATIM from layout.html (the markup is byte-identical, so rendering does not change). It also exists as a second, hand-copied copy in pages/facturas/factura-standalone.html, which does not include layout.html because the POS opens in its own browser window. That copy is left untouched in W1 and folded in during W6. WHY A FRAGMENT AND NOT A PARTIAL The inline style carries the positioning. It cannot move into web/mercurius.scss without changing specificity relationships against the Bulma .notification rules, and the POS copy must stay in sync with it. One include, one definition. DATA CONTRACT (unchanged) "toasts" - optional list of {severity, message}. When absent the safe expression yields null and nothing renders, so pages that pass no toasts pay nothing. See fragments/toasts.html for the severity vocabulary (error -> is-danger, warn -> is-warning, success -> is-success, else is-info) and _kit/toast-item.html for HTMX-swapped toasts. WHO APPENDS TO IT - Server render: a resource passes `toasts` in the data model. - HTMX swap: any response fragment wraps the include in an out-of-band swap. This is how every mutation in the app reports success:
{#include _kit/toast-item severity=result.severity message=result.message /}
Do not add a second container, and do not reposition it per page. ACCESSIBILITY aria-live="polite" + aria-atomic="false" so appended notifications are announced without re-reading the whole stack. Toasts are role="alert" (assertive) at the item level, which is correct for error/success outcomes but means a burst of toasts will interrupt - keep them one at a time. Every toast auto-dismisses after 5s and carries a .delete button (aria-label="Cerrar notificacion"). !}
